What to check before ordering

  • Listing and variant. Save the item ID and confirm the seller link, color, size and variant price. Check whether your buying agent accepts the seller link.
  • Measurements. Measure a similar garment laid flat and compare its chest width, length and sleeve length with the seller's chart. For shoes, use the seller's foot-length or insole chart and check which measurement it uses.
  • Quality-control photos. When order-specific QC photos are available, compare the label, color and measurements with your selected variant. Ask for close-ups of any unclear seams, soles or labels before approving shipment.
  • Delivered cost. Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent and payment fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Compare shipping quotes using the same destination, packed dimensions and weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a way to organize product finds into a shortlist for shopping research. This independent guide draws its product listings from HipoBuyFind. Confirm link support directly with the agent you plan to use.

Where do the two buttons go?

Both open the HipoBuyFind homepage. The individual product cards open their corresponding HipoBuyFind listing pages.

How can I tell whether a photo shows my exact item?

Check that the photo belongs to your order and that the visible color, size label and measurements match your chosen variant. Catalog photos are for browsing.

Why can a shipping quote change after packing?

Some routes charge using dimensional weight when it exceeds the parcel's actual weight. Use the final packed dimensions and weight to compare available routes to your destination.
